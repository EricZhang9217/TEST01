Central nervous system: four mechanisms, three shared gates
Four disease mechanisms read left to right, target cell through to boundary. Brown = the intervention and payload. Every mechanism must clear all three gates above separately.
Three independent gates, shared by all four
1Cross the BBB  2Enter parenchymal target cell
3Cover the correct brain regions
One gate's evidence cannot replace another's
Key methodological caveat
Homogenate knockdown is a weighted sum: KD_bulk = Σ wi × KDi
wi = share of target RNA, not of cells → 75% bulk knockdown ≠ 75% of neurons reached
