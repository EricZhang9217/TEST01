The part AI cannot replace today

Functional-delivery validation: receptor-dependent productive intracellular payload and sequence-specific PD, without transfection. A high-affinity binder, a differentially expressed gene or an in vitro active sequence is not a usable AOC.

Best training data: uptake, productive payload, PD, function, toxicity and manufacturability for the same candidate in diseased and normal cells — failed candidates kept.
