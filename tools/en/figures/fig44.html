Conservative tier — buy discrimination read-outs first
First: backbone plus delivery study. Buys: uptake on two axes and functional potency, with four entry-dependence controls. Downside: non-binding control works → entry hypothesis goes back.
Aggressive tier — buy functional-chain read-outs first
First: delivery study and patient-cell arm in one batch. Buys: the four-level chain in patient myotubes. Needs: ≥3 LOPD donors, matched normals. Downside: RNA but no protein.
Authorisation boundary: gates stop at receptor entry & payload fit, including patient-cell biology; platform transfer and all after it return separately. One capability is worth owning across all three configurations — site-specific conjugation plus full OAR characterisation.
